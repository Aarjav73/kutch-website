How Your RFQ Is Processed

Bhagwati Chemicals follows a rigorous four-step commercial procedure for every inquiry:

  1. Verification: Commercial Desk validates volume, delivery port, and technical grade specs.
  2. Plant Scheduling: Inquiry is transmitted directly to our plant dispatch units (Gandhidham / Jhagadia / Dahej).
  3. Spot Pricing Calculation: Current feedstock parity price is calculated without intermediary markups.
  4. Quotation Return: A formal Proforma / Spot Quotation with batch COA is delivered within 1 business day.
