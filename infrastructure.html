Manufacturing Facilities • Industrial Complexes

World-Scale Chemical Infrastructure

Explore our continuous process manufacturing complexes, captive Chlor-Alkali facilities, and environmental engineering systems powering our supply chain across Gujarat.

Core Synthesis Unit

Chlor-Alkali & Membrane Cell Plant

State-of-the-art zero-mercury, bipolar membrane cell technology producing Caustic Soda Lye (48-50%), Caustic Soda Flakes (99.5%), Liquid Chlorine in certified tonners, and compressed ultra-pure Hydrogen gas. Direct captive feedstocks provide complete downstream price immunity.

Outputs: Caustic Soda Lye • Caustic Flakes • Chlorine Liquid • Hydrogen Gas
Continuous Synthesis

Continuous Nitration & Chlorination Towers

Continuous benzene and toluene chlorination reactors integrated with automated adiabatic nitration units. Equipped with sophisticated DCS control instrumentation to guarantee isomeric purity across ODCB, PDCB, TCB, Nitro Benzene, and DCNB derivatives.

Outputs: ODCB • PDCB • 1,2,4-TCB • PNCB • ONCB • 2,4-DNCB
High-Pressure Technology

Catalytic Hydrogenation Autoclaves

High-pressure catalytic hydrogenation autoclaves utilizing captive hydrogen from the chlor-alkali plant. Engineered for the selective reduction of nitro-aromatics into premium grade Para Amino Phenol (PAP), Paracetamol, and aromatic amines for pharma synthesis.

Outputs: Para Amino Phenol • Paracetamol Active • Anisidines • DCA isomers
Specialty Fractionation

Acid Chlorides & High-Vacuum Distillation

Glass-lined and hastelloy reactor systems with high-vacuum fractional distillation columns dedicated exclusively to specialty acid chlorides. Eliminates trace metals and guarantees low-color, high-assay intermediates for peroxides and agrochemical synthesis.

Outputs: Neodecanoyl Chloride • 2-Ethylhexanoyl • Pivaloyl Chloride
Heavy Chemical Backbone

Sulphuric Acid & Oleum Manufacturing

Double Contact Double Absorption (DCDA) contact process plants producing commercial Sulphuric Acid 98%, Oleum 23% and 65%, Sulphur Dioxide gas, and Sodium Hydrosulphite reducing agents used heavily in textile denim processing and dyestuff manufacturing.

Outputs: Sulphuric Acid 98% • Oleum 23%/65% • Sodium Hydrosulphite • SO2
Environmental Regimes

Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) & QA Labs

Full regulatory compliance with biological ETP, Multiple Effect Evaporators (MEE), and agitated thin film dryers (ATFD). Central analytical laboratories fitted with GC-MS, HPLC, spectrophotometers, and Karl Fischer titrators to ensure strict lot-to-lot consistency.

Standards: ISO 9001:2015 • ISO 14001 • Zero Liquid Discharge • GLC Certified

Dedicated Bulk Tanker Logistics & Port Access

Direct proximity to Mundra and Kandla ports allows rapid container stuffing and maritime export dispatch. Domestic orders are fulfilled via our dedicated fleet of GPS-tracked road tankers and certified container carriers across India.
