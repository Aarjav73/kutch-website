Our Enterprise Heritage

Reliability, Pure Chemistry & Direct Supply

With more than 40 years of market experience, Bhagwati Chemicals has established an unshakeable reputation for honest business practices, consistent batch quality, and timely nationwide and international dispatch.

Our manufacturing operations encompass continuous reaction chlorination, adiabatic nitration, catalytic hydrogenation, and bulk sulphuric acid production complexes situated in Gujarat's premier chemical corridors (Gandhidham, Jhagadia, and Dahej).

Whether catering to spot requirements or multi-year enterprise contracts, our Ahmedabad commercial headquarters works in lockstep with our production plants to ensure strict analytical compliance and transparent pricing.

Key Operational Strengths:

  • ✓ Direct Plant Allocation: Immediate batch scheduling from high-capacity continuous production units.
  • ✓ Transparent Spot & Formula Pricing: Real-time pricing linked directly to raw material feedstock indices.
  • ✓ Dedicated Multi-Hub Fleet: Captive fleet of insulated stainless steel and mild steel tankers for hazardous bulk transit.
